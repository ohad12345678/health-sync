Privacy policy

health-sync is a private tool used only by its owner.

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's own Google Health activity and heart-rate data.
  • Data is stored only on the owner's personal computer and is not shared, sold, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or any purpose other than the owner's personal fitness tracking.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
